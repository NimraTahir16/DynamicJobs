<h1>Dynamic Jobs Dashboard</h1>

 

<h2>Description</h2>
Dynamic Jobs Dashboard <br>

<h4>Project Overview</h4>

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built on data from Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. It serves as a centralized management tool to monitor active client jobs, evaluate project profitability, track work-in-progress expenses, and maintain financial control from project initiation to completion. The report transforms operational and accounting data into clear visuals, helping managers spot cost overruns, track billing status, and make data-driven decisions.

<h4>Key Features</h4>

Monitors average job completion rates and visualizes project lifecycle stages (In Progress, Completed, On Hold, and Not Started).
Summarized recognized sales, costs, and profit figures alongside overall profit percentages across individual jobs and customer accounts.
Compared remaining project costs against expected remaining revenue per job to highlight financial risk before delivery
Detailed item quantities ordered, received, invoiced, and posted per job to prevent supply chain delays.
Features a modular set of granular slicers (by Job, Customer, Salesperson, Task, and Date Range) for targeted analysis


Business Value: Provided a clear overview of sales performance and helped identify growth opportunities, high-performing products, and areas for improvement.
